Step-by-Step Guide

How to Install an M.2 SSD in Your PS5

Takes about 10 minutes. All you need is a small #1 Phillips screwdriver. Works on both PS5 Standard and PS5 Slim.

What you need: A compatible PCIe Gen 4 NVMe M.2 SSD (see our top picks), a heatsink (many SSDs include one), and a #1 Phillips screwdriver.
1

Power off and unplug your PS5 completely

Hold the power button until it beeps twice and the light turns off. Unplug all cables including the power cable. Wait 3 minutes to let the internals cool down.

2

Lay the PS5 flat on a clean surface

Place it with the disc drive facing down (or the flat side down for Digital Edition). A microfibre cloth underneath helps prevent scratches.

3

Remove the side panel

Grip the top corner of the white panel and slide it towards the bottom of the console, then lift up. It unclips with a gentle pop, don't force it.

4

Locate and uncover the M.2 expansion slot

Find the small rectangular metal cover near the fan. Remove its single #1 Phillips screw and lift the cover off. This reveals the M.2 SSD expansion slot.

5

Prepare your SSD with a heatsink

A heatsink is mandatory, Sony requires it and thermal throttling will degrade performance without one. If your SSD came with one, attach it now using the included thermal pad. The total height of SSD + heatsink must not exceed 11.25mm to fit the PS5 bay.

6

Insert the SSD at a 45° angle

Align the notch on the SSD with the M.2 slot and slide it in at about a 45-degree angle. You'll feel it seat into the connector. Don't force it.

7

Press down and secure with the screw

Gently press the SSD flat and secure it with the small screw. Don't overtighten. Replace the slot cover and its screw, then reattach the side panel.

8

Power on and format

Plug in and turn on your PS5. It will detect the new SSD and prompt you to format it, this takes about 2 minutes. Done! Your expansion drive will appear in Storage Settings and games can be installed directly to it.
