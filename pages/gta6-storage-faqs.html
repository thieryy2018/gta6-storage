Common Questions

GTA 6 Storage FAQ

Every question about GTA 6 storage, answered with facts from official Sony, Microsoft, and Rockstar sources.

The Basics

How big is GTA 6?

GTA 6 is reported to require approximately 210GB of storage on PS5, one of the largest game installs ever. For context, Red Dead Redemption 2 was around 105GB, so GTA 6 is roughly double. This figure may increase with day-one patches and future updates, so leave extra headroom.

When does GTA 6 launch?

GTA 6 launches on November 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC release date confirmed yet. Sony has confirmed PS5 as the "Plays Best On" platform.

When does GTA 6 pre-loading start?

Pre-loading begins on November 10, 2026, nine days before the November 19 launch. You will need storage space clear and your upgrade installed before that date to start the download immediately.

Will GTA 6 fit on my PS5 without an upgrade?

The PS5 has approximately 667GB of usable storage (from a 1TB drive). GTA 6 needs 210GB. If your console is completely empty, it fits, but most people have games installed. If you have more than 457GB of games, you will need to delete some or add an M.2 SSD upgrade. The PS5 Slim has the same usable storage.

Will GTA 6 fit on Xbox Series S?

Barely. The Xbox Series S has only 364GB of usable storage. GTA 6 at 210GB would take up 58% of the entire console, leaving just 154GB for everything else. If you have any other games installed, you will need to delete them or add a Seagate Storage Expansion Card before pre-loading starts.

PS5 Storage

What SSD do I need for PS5?

According to Sony's official guidance, you need an M.2 NVMe SSD using PCIe Gen 4 (Key M interface). Sony recommends at least 5,500MB/s sequential read speed. A heatsink is required, Sony states it is mandatory. M.2 SATA SSDs are not supported. The SSD can be between 250GB and 8TB capacity. All SSDs on our recommended list meet these specifications.

Do I need a heatsink with my PS5 SSD?

Yes. Sony's official guidance requires a heatsink or heat transfer sheet. Without one, your SSD will thermally throttle and perform poorly in GTA 6's demanding open world. Many recommended SSDs include a heatsink in the box. If yours does not, a compatible standalone heatsink like the Sabrent PS5 unit can be found for under £15.

Can I use an external USB SSD for GTA 6 on PS5?

No. This is one of the most important facts to know. According to Sony's official support pages, "PlayStation 5 games cannot be played directly from USB extended storage." GTA 6 (a PS5 game) must be installed on either the PS5's internal SSD or an M.2 expansion drive. A USB drive will not work for playing GTA 6 on PS5. See our full PS5 storage guide for the complete picture.

What CAN I use an external USB drive for on PS5?

USB external drives on PS5 are useful but limited. You can store and play PS4 games directly from USB extended storage. You can also store PS5 game data on USB and copy it back to internal or M.2 storage when you want to play. This makes USB drives useful for archiving PS4 titles to free up your internal drive for GTA 6, not for playing GTA 6 itself. Sony requires SuperSpeed USB (5Gbps minimum) and a drive between 250GB and 8TB.

Can PS5 games run from the M.2 expansion slot?

Yes. Sony confirms that PS5 games are fully playable from M.2 SSD storage. This is the correct upgrade path for GTA 6. An M.2 SSD installed in the PS5's expansion bay performs at the same level as the internal storage. Sony does note that "not all games are necessarily playable with the exact same performance as the internal Ultra-High Speed SSD," but in practice quality Gen 4 SSDs perform identically for gaming.

How long does it take to install a PS5 SSD?

The physical installation takes about 5 to 10 minutes with a #1 Phillips screwdriver. The PS5 will then prompt you to format the new drive, which takes around 2 minutes. See our full step-by-step install guide for the complete process.

Xbox Storage

What storage options does Xbox Series X|S support?

Xbox Series X|S uses a proprietary Storage Expansion Card that plugs into a dedicated port on the back of the console. Microsoft states these cards "replicate the console's custom SSD experience" and match internal SSD performance exactly. Xbox also supports USB 3.1 external drives, but these have important limitations for Series X|S games.

Can I use a USB SSD for GTA 6 on Xbox?

Almost certainly not. Xbox Series X|S games require the internal SSD or the proprietary Storage Expansion Card to run. Microsoft's official pages describe USB support as being for "external HDD" use. While you can store Xbox Series X|S game data on a USB drive, you would need to move it back to internal or expansion card storage before playing. For GTA 6 on Xbox, the Seagate Storage Expansion Card is the only reliable option for adding playable storage.

Can I play backwards-compatible games from USB on Xbox?

USB drives on Xbox work best for Xbox One, Xbox 360, and original Xbox backwards-compatible games. These older-generation titles can typically be stored and played from external USB storage. Xbox Series X|S optimised games (like GTA 6) require the internal SSD or expansion card.

What is the Seagate Storage Expansion Card?

The Seagate Storage Expansion Card is an official Xbox-licensed storage device that plugs into the proprietary expansion port on the back of Xbox Series X|S consoles. Microsoft confirms it "matches internal storage performance." It is currently the only way to expand storage for Xbox Series X|S games. It comes in 512GB, 1TB, and 2TB sizes. The 2TB version is the best value for GTA 6.

Does Xbox Series S support M.2 SSDs?

No. Neither the Xbox Series S nor the Xbox Series X have M.2 expansion slots. Xbox uses its own proprietary expansion port that only accepts official Storage Expansion Cards. If you see guides suggesting M.2 SSDs for Xbox, they are incorrect. The only expansion option for Xbox Series X|S is the official Seagate card.

PC and Other Platforms

Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Not at launch. GTA 6 releases exclusively on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on November 19, 2026. A PC version has not been officially confirmed by Rockstar Games, but is widely expected sometime in 2027 based on the pattern set by previous GTA releases. See our PC storage guide for what to expect when it does arrive.

What storage will GTA 6 need on PC?

Official PC requirements have not been announced yet. Based on the 210GB PS5 figure and typical PC game compression, expect somewhere between 150GB and 220GB on PC. PC players will want an NVMe SSD for best performance, GTA 6 will likely take advantage of DirectStorage on PC, which requires NVMe. We will update our PC guide as soon as requirements are confirmed.
