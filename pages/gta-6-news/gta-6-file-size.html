GTA 6 File Size: Everything You Need to Know About the 210GB Install

9 November 2026 GTA6Storage.co.uk

The Headline Figure: 210GB

GTA 6 is shaping up to be approximately 210GB on PS5. That's the base install — before day-one patches, before updates, before any additional content Rockstar pushes post-launch. Expect the real-world footprint to creep higher once you've actually downloaded everything on release day.

Rockstar hasn't officially confirmed the exact number, but leaked pre-load data points firmly to 210GB. Given the sheer scale of the game — a living, breathing recreation of a fictional Florida spanning cities, suburbs, and swampland — it's not surprising. What is surprising is just how much larger it is than anything that's come before it.

How GTA 6 Stacks Up Against Other Massive Games

To put 210GB in context, here's how GTA 6 compares to some of the biggest installs on current-gen consoles.

GTA 6 is nearly double Red Dead Redemption 2 and more than twice Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III. If your console was full after those games, GTA 6 is going to cause real problems.

What This Means for Each Console

PS5 (667GB usable)

GTA 6 eats up 31% of a completely empty PS5's usable storage, leaving you with 457GB. The catch? Almost nobody has an empty console. The average PS5 owner has 200–300GB of games installed. Add GTA 6 on top of that and you're looking at 90–120GB of headroom at best — barely enough for one or two more titles.

Xbox Series X (802GB usable)

The best-positioned current-gen console. After GTA 6 you'd have 592GB remaining on a fresh machine. Still not infinite, but Xbox Series X owners are in the least precarious position of anyone at launch.

Xbox Series S (364GB usable)

This is where things get genuinely rough. GTA 6 consumes 58% of the Xbox Series S's total usable storage, leaving just 154GB. On a console that was already tight on space, that's essentially no room for anything else. If you're on a Series S, you need to act now.

Will GTA 6 Actually Fit on Your PS5?

Simple maths: if you've already got more than 457GB installed on your PS5, GTA 6 won't fit without deleting something. Most PS5 owners will need to clear at least a few games before pre-loading begins.

The key date is 12 November 2026 — that's when pre-loading starts. You need your storage sorted before then. If you're waiting until 19 November to sort it out, you'll be watching the launch stream while your console slowly downloads, well behind everyone else.

What About Xbox Series S?

The Series S is the most affected console by far. With only 364GB usable, GTA 6 takes up more than half the available storage before you've installed a single other game. And unlike PS5, there's no cheap M.2 SSD fix — the Series S uses proprietary Seagate expansion cards that slot into a port on the back of the console. They're more expensive per gigabyte than M.2 drives, but they're the only option.

What About the PC Version?

There's no PC release confirmed for 19 November — GTA 6 is launching on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. When PC does eventually arrive, the file size could be different depending on texture quality settings and compression. PC M.2 SSDs are also significantly cheaper than PS5-specific drives, so storage won't be the headache it is on console.

Quick Answers

How big is GTA 6?

Approximately 210GB on PS5 as a base install. This may increase with day-one patches and future updates, so leave extra headroom.

When does GTA 6 pre-loading start in the UK?

Pre-loading starts on 12 November 2026 — one week before the 19 November launch. You need your storage upgrade installed before that date to start downloading straight away.

What happens if GTA 6 doesn't fit on my PS5?

You'll need to delete existing games to free up space, or add an M.2 SSD expansion drive to your PS5. The WD_BLACK SN850P 2TB is Sony's officially licensed option and takes about 10 minutes to install.
