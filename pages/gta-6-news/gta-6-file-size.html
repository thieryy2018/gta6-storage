GTA 6 File Size: Everything You Need to Know About the 210GB Install

9 November 2026 GTA6Storage.co.uk

The Headline Figure: 210GB

GTA 6 is shaping up to be approximately 210GB on PS5. That's the base install - before day-one patches, before updates, before any additional content Rockstar Games pushes post-launch. Expect the real-world footprint to creep higher once you've actually downloaded everything on release day. Plan for 250GB or more if you're thinking long-term.

Rockstar hasn't officially confirmed the exact number, but leaked pre-load data points firmly to 210GB. Given the sheer scale of the game - a living, breathing recreation of a fictional Florida spanning cities, suburbs, and swampland - it's not surprising. What is surprising is just how much larger it is than anything that's come before it.

How GTA 6 Compares to Other Massive Games

To put 210GB in context, here's how GTA 6 stacks up against some of the largest installs on current-gen consoles.

GTA 6 is nearly double Red Dead Redemption 2 and more than twice the size of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare III. If your console was full after those games, GTA 6 is going to cause real problems.

What This Means for Each Console

PS5 (667GB usable)

GTA 6 eats 31% of a completely empty PS5's usable storage, leaving 457GB. The catch? Almost nobody has an empty console. The average PS5 owner has 200–300GB of games installed. Add GTA 6 on top and you're looking at 90–120GB of headroom at best — barely enough for one or two more titles.

Xbox Series X (802GB usable)

The best-positioned current-gen console. After installing GTA 6 you'd have 592GB remaining on a fresh machine. Xbox Series X owners are in the least precarious position at launch, though that buffer will erode quickly as more Series X titles ship.

Xbox Series S (364GB usable)

This is where things get genuinely rough. GTA 6 consumes 58% of the Xbox Series S's total usable storage, leaving just 154GB. On a console that was already tight on space, that's essentially no room for anything else. If you're on a Series S, the time to act is now - not 18 November.

Will GTA 6 Actually Fit on Your PS5?

Simple maths: if you've already got more than 457GB installed, GTA 6 won't fit without deleting something. Most PS5 owners will need to clear at least a few games before pre-loading kicks off.

The key date is 12 November 2026 — that's when pre-loading starts. You need your storage sorted before then. Waiting until 19 November means watching the launch whilst your console slowly downloads, well behind everyone else.

The Easiest Fix: PS5 SSD Upgrade

Rather than playing an increasingly painful game of whack-a-mole deleting old games, the proper solution is an M.2 SSD expansion drive. Sony's official guide confirms the PS5 supports M.2 NVMe drives up to 4TB, and installation takes around 10 minutes with a small Phillips screwdriver. A 2TB drive gives you space for GTA 6 and 15-20 more large games.

What About Xbox Series S?

The Series S is the most affected console by far. With only 364GB usable, GTA 6 takes up more than half the available storage before you've installed a single other game. And unlike PS5, there's no cheap M.2 fix - the Series S uses proprietary Seagate expansion cards that slot into a port on the back. They're more expensive per gigabyte than M.2 drives, but they're the only option for adding playable storage.

What About the PC Version?

There's no PC release confirmed for 19 November - GTA 6 is launching on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only. When PC does eventually arrive, the file size could differ depending on texture pack choices. PC M.2 SSDs are significantly cheaper than PS5-specific drives too, so storage will be less of a headache when it lands on PC.

GTA 6 File Size — Frequently Asked Questions

The Basics

How big is GTA 6?

GTA 6 is approximately 210GB on PS5 as a base install. This figure may increase with day-one patches and future updates — plan for 250GB or more in the long run.

Has Rockstar officially confirmed the GTA 6 file size?

No. Rockstar Games has not officially confirmed the exact file size. The 210GB figure comes from leaked pre-load data that has been widely reported across the games press. The actual install may differ slightly, and day-one patches will add to the total.

Will GTA 6 get bigger over time?

Almost certainly. The 210GB figure covers the base install only. Day-one patches, online content updates, story DLC, and future expansions will push the total higher over time. If you're buying storage specifically for GTA 6, plan for 250GB minimum.

Is the GTA 6 file size the same on Xbox as on PS5?

The Xbox Series X|S file size hasn't been officially confirmed and may differ slightly. The Xbox version typically compresses assets differently to PS5. The 210GB figure currently cited relates to the PS5 version.

PS5 Storage

Will GTA 6 fit on my PS5 without deleting anything?

Only if you have at least 210GB of free space. The PS5 has approximately 667GB of usable storage. If you have more than 457GB already installed, GTA 6 won't fit without deleting games or adding an M.2 SSD upgrade. Use our storage checker →

When does GTA 6 pre-loading start in the UK?

Pre-loading starts on 12 November 2026, one week before the 19 November launch. You need your storage sorted and any upgrade installed before that date to start downloading straight away.

What do I do if GTA 6 doesn't fit on my PS5?

You have two options: delete games to free up space, or add an M.2 SSD expansion drive. The M.2 upgrade is the cleaner fix — a 2TB drive gives you space for GTA 6 and 15–20 more large games, and takes about 10 minutes to install. See our full install guide →

Xbox Storage

Will GTA 6 fit on Xbox Series S?

With difficulty. The Xbox Series S has only 364GB of usable storage. GTA 6 at 210GB takes up 58% of the entire console, leaving just 154GB for everything else. Most Series S owners will need to delete games or add a Seagate Storage Expansion Card before pre-loading.

Can Xbox Series S use an M.2 SSD like PS5?

No. Xbox Series X|S uses a proprietary Seagate expansion card that plugs into a dedicated port on the back. Standard M.2 SSDs do not work. The expansion card is more expensive per gigabyte than a PS5 M.2 drive, but it's the only way to add playable storage to a Series S.
