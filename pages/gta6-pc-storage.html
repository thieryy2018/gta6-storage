GTA 6 PC Storage: What to Expect

GTA 6 launches on consoles November 19, 2026. A PC version has not been officially announced yet, but it is widely expected. Here is what PC players need to know about storage now, so you are ready when it arrives.

PC Release: Not Announced Yet

As of November 2026, Rockstar Games has not confirmed a PC release date for GTA 6. The console launch (PS5 and Xbox Series X|S) is November 19. Based on Rockstar's history with GTA V, a PC version typically follows the console launch by 12 to 18 months. We will update this page as soon as official information is released.

Why an NVMe SSD Matters for GTA 6 on PC

GTA 6's world is designed from the ground up around the fast storage in PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. These consoles have SSDs capable of delivering data at 5,500MB/s and higher. When Rockstar ports GTA 6 to PC, they will almost certainly require an NVMe SSD to replicate that streaming performance.

SATA SSDs (which max out around 550MB/s) and mechanical hard drives will likely either be unsupported or result in significant performance problems, pop-in, stuttering, and slower load times. Getting an NVMe SSD now means you will be ready on day one.
