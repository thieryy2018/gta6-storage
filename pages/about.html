What This Site Is

GTA6Storage.co.uk is an independent website dedicated to helping console gamers prepare for GTA 6. We cover the storage requirements, the best PS5 M.2 SSDs, Xbox Series expansion cards, and step-by-step installation guides. Our goal is simple: make sure you are ready on launch day, with no last-minute scramble.

The site is run by a small team of UK-based gamers who got burned by storage issues on previous big launches and decided to do something about it.
