Xbox Storage Guide

Xbox Series X|S Storage for GTA 6

Xbox does not use M.2 SSDs. Series S owners have only 364GB total. Here is what you actually need, and what will not work, before pre-loading starts on November 10.

M.2 SSDs

Not compatible. Xbox Series X|S has no M.2 slot. Any guide suggesting M.2 for Xbox is wrong.

USB Drives for GTA 6

Will not work for GTA 6. Xbox Series X|S titles cannot run from USB. Only backwards-compatible games can.

Expansion Card

The correct solution. Official Seagate Storage Expansion Card. Matches internal SSD performance exactly. Plug-and-play.

Why Xbox Storage Is Different from PS5

Microsoft designed the Xbox Series X|S around a proprietary storage architecture called Xbox Velocity Architecture. This system is baked into the custom SSD and the expansion port, it is what enables features like Quick Resume and instant world-loading.

Because of this, Xbox cannot simply use off-the-shelf M.2 drives the way PS5 can. The only way to expand storage for Xbox Series X|S games is with an official Storage Expansion Card designed to work within this architecture.

What You Can Use USB Drives For on Xbox

External USB drives are not useless on Xbox, they just have limits. Here is what they can and cannot do:

USB CAN do this on Xbox
  • Store and play Xbox One games
  • Store and play Xbox 360 backwards-compatible games
  • Store and play original Xbox backwards-compatible games
  • Archive Xbox Series X|S game data (move back to SSD to play)
USB CANNOT do this on Xbox
  • Run GTA 6 (Xbox Series X|S version)
  • Run any Xbox Series X|S optimised game
  • Replace the Storage Expansion Card
  • Match internal SSD performance
